# Folhas do elenco

Cada personagem numa linha, 8 posições por linha. Para uma folha maior de um só personagem, deixe só a linha dele em CHARACTERS.

## Folha 1: 8 ângulos

```
Create a professional 2D character sheet of the whole cast, with each character in one horizontal row, one below the other, from top to bottom, in the exact order listed.

MASTER CHARACTER STYLE — MANDATORY

All characters must follow the exact same visual identity:

ART STYLE:

- Dark Cinematic Lo-Fi Cartoon 2D.
- Hand-painted digital illustration.
- Mature graphic novel aesthetic.
- Clean, slightly textured dark outlines.
- Soft painterly shading with subtle brush textures.
- Muted, desaturated colors.
- Cold blue-gray and dusty purple undertones.
- Detailed clothing textures, folds and shadows.
- Consistent rendering quality across all characters.

HEAD AND FACE — CRITICAL:

- Perfectly round, smooth, spherical cartoon head.
- Soft off-white, slightly grayish face color.
- Muted porcelain-white appearance, never pure bright white.
- Subtle cool-gray and lavender shadows around the edges.
- Gentle shading to emphasize the spherical shape.
- Small black oval eyes.
- Minimalist black eyebrows.
- Small, simple mouth, always visible in front and three-quarter views.
- No realistic nose, ears or human facial anatomy.
- Hair and facial hair may vary between characters.
- Preserve the same head shape and face color across the entire series.

BODY AND SKIN:

- All visible skin must use the same muted off-white grayish tone as the face.
- No natural human skin tones.
- Soft gray-lavender shadows on arms, hands, legs and neck.
- Never use pure white or glowing skin.
- Anatomically appropriate body proportions.
- Adults: approximately 6.5–7 heads tall.
- Children: age-appropriate proportions, approximately 5–5.5 heads tall.
- No chibi proportions.

COLOR AND LIGHTING:

- Muted, low-saturation color palette.
- Soft cinematic ambient lighting.
- Moderate contrast.
- Subtle cool shadows.
- No harsh highlights on white surfaces.
- No excessive brightness or glossy effects.

CHARACTER CONSISTENCY:

- Identical head shape and skin tone for every character.
- Consistent facial feature style.
- Consistent line thickness and shading technique.
- Hairstyles, clothing, accessories and body proportions may vary.
- Preserve each character's established appearance across scenes.

CHARACTERS, ONE ROW EACH:
Row 1: João, a 25-year-old male delivery worker with a slim, naturally proportioned adult body. Approximately 7 heads tall; Short, messy dark hair with visible volume; Subtle dark stubble and short beard; Serious, slightly tired expression. Wearing: Dark charcoal-gray delivery jacket with a hood; Subtle reflective gray stripe across the chest and sleeves; Black undershirt; Dark cargo pants with realistic fabric folds; Black fingerless gloves; Dark rugged sneakers or boots; Large, worn, dark-red insulated delivery backpack with reflective details.
Row 2: Geraldo, a 58-year-old male farmer and former truck driver with a tall, lean, weathered adult body. Approximately 7 heads tall; Short gray hair under the hat; Thick gray mustache; Stern, weary expression. Wearing: Worn straw hat; Faded brown plaid button-up shirt with rolled sleeves; Dark work pants with realistic fabric folds; Old leather belt; Scuffed brown work boots; Small handheld CB radio clipped to the belt.
Row 3: Cida, a 54-year-old female nursing technician with a medium, naturally proportioned adult body. Approximately 7 heads tall; Dark hair with gray strands tied in a low bun; Calm, exhausted expression. Wearing: Muted green hospital scrubs, top and pants, with realistic fabric folds; Worn gray cardigan over the scrubs; ID badge on a lanyard; Worn white nursing sneakers, slightly dirty; Small dark first-aid pouch at the waist.
Row 4: Bia, a 27-year-old female veterinarian with an athletic, naturally proportioned adult body. Approximately 7 heads tall; Dark braids tied back, with visible volume; Dry, determined expression. Wearing: Muted blue work vest with many pockets; Faded gray T-shirt; Dark gray work pants with realistic fabric folds; Brown rugged boots; Worn veterinary bag on a shoulder strap.
Row 5: Téo, a 9-year-old boy with a naturally proportioned child body, not chibi. Approximately 5.5 heads tall; Short dark hair showing under the cap; Curious, watchful expression. Wearing: Faded yellow baseball cap; Worn off-white soccer T-shirt, slightly too big; Muted blue shorts; Dirty white sneakers; Small dark backpack.
Row 6: Dona Zefa, a 79-year-old woman with a short, thin, slightly hunched elderly body. Approximately 6.5 heads tall; White hair tied in a small bun; Round glasses; Soft wrinkles drawn with minimal lines; Stubborn, watchful expression. Wearing: Faded purple floral dress with realistic fabric folds; Worn dark knitted cardigan; Dark flat shoes; Wooden walking cane; Small rosary around the wrist.
Row 7: Nei, a 55-year-old male truck driver with a heavyset, naturally proportioned adult body and a large belly. Approximately 7 heads tall; Broad shoulders; Short dark hair under the cap; Dark full beard; Loud, friendly expression that hides worry. Wearing: Dark gray worn cap; Burnt-orange work shirt with rolled sleeves, sweat-stained; Dark jeans with realistic fabric folds; Old leather belt with a large buckle; Scuffed work boots; Heavy key ring on the belt.
Row 8: Dandara, a 30-year-old female cook with a strong, naturally proportioned adult body. Approximately 7 heads tall; Large natural black afro hair with visible volume; Direct, unimpressed expression. Wearing: Dark red bandana tied around the hair; Dirty off-white chef jacket with sleeves pushed up; Black work pants with realistic fabric folds; Black non-slip kitchen shoes; Worn apron tied at the waist, with a pocket holding an old phone.
Row 9: Dárcio Leme, a 60-year-old male meatpacking plant owner and radio host with a tall, thin, naturally proportioned adult body. Approximately 7 heads tall; Neat short white hair; Gentle, unsettling smile. Wearing: Worn beige three-piece suit with realistic fabric folds; Dark red tie; Brown fedora hat; Polished brown leather shoes; Old gold watch on the wrist.
Row 10: A "repetidor", an infected ordinary adult from a Brazilian town, with a thin, naturally proportioned adult body. Approximately 7 heads tall; Same round head and skin as everyone, slightly grayer; Messy, thinning dark hair; Empty white eyes; Slightly open mouth, as if repeating words; Blank, hollow expression and a slightly crooked posture. Wearing: Dirty, torn everyday T-shirt with realistic fabric folds; Stained work pants; One missing shoe and one worn sneaker; No visible blood or gore.

COLUMNS, THE SAME 8 POSITIONS IN EVERY ROW, FROM LEFT TO RIGHT:
1. front view.
2. front three-quarter view facing right.
3. right side profile.
4. back three-quarter view facing right.
5. back view.
6. back three-quarter view facing left.
7. left side profile.
8. front three-quarter view facing left.

COMPOSITION:
A clean grid with one full-body figure per cell, evenly spaced, none overlapping or cropped, same scale for every adult. Keep every character's body, clothing, colors and accessories identical in all 8 positions. Plain light-gray background with very subtle grounding shadows. Vertical composition, 2:3 aspect ratio.

NEGATIVE PROMPT:
Pure white skin, glowing white face, bright white limbs, realistic skin tones, human facial anatomy, realistic nose, photorealistic face, 3D render, Pixar style, anime, manga, chibi, oversized head, short adult legs, flat vector art, plastic skin, overly saturated colors, inconsistent character design, text, logos, watermark.
```
