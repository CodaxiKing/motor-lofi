# Fichas de referência dos personagens

Gere uma por personagem e anexe no ChatGPT junto com cada prompt de pose, para o rosto, o cabelo e a roupa saírem sempre iguais. Salve como `ref_<personagem>.png`.

Create a professional 2D character model sheet in a Dark Cinematic Lo-Fi Cartoon style, inspired by atmospheric survival horror animated storytelling.

ART STYLE:

- Hand-painted 2D digital illustration.
- Dark cinematic cartoon aesthetic.
- Semi-realistic adult body proportions.
- Minimalist cartoon facial features.
- Clean, slightly textured dark outlines.
- Soft painterly shading with subtle brush textures.
- Muted, desaturated colors.

- Detailed clothing folds and fabric textures.
- Atmospheric, melancholic visual identity.
- Mature illustrated graphic novel appearance.
- Consistent character design across all views.

CHARACTER:
João, a 25-year-old male delivery worker with a slim, naturally proportioned adult body.

- Approximately 7 heads tall.
- Long, naturally proportioned legs.
- Normal adult shoulder width.
- Round, smooth, white cartoon face.
- Short, messy dark hair with visible volume.
- Subtle dark stubble and short beard.
- Small black oval eyes.
- Minimalist expressive eyebrows.
- Small, simplified mouth.
- Serious, slightly tired expression.

CLOTHING:

- Dark charcoal-gray delivery jacket with a hood.
- Subtle reflective gray stripe across the chest and sleeves.
- Black undershirt.
- Dark cargo pants with realistic fabric folds.
- Black fingerless gloves.
- Dark rugged sneakers or boots.
- Large, worn, dark-red insulated delivery backpack with reflective details.

CHARACTER SHEET COMPOSITION:
